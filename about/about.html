OUR STORY

Abhath Science and Technology, a start-up company founded in 2020, seeks to launch products in the field of health, environment, and energy in the Libyan market to contribute to supporting the industry in Libya and creating a knowledge-based economy while reducing reliance on oil.

OUR VISION

To be the leading manufacturer and developer of products that enhance people's quality of life through partnerships, innovation and growth, we strive to become the company of choice for all those we serve employees, customers, suppliers, society and investors.

OUR MISSION

Launching products and services of the highest quality and in innovative ways that meet the needs of the market and the expectations of our distinguished customers in the field of health, environment, and energy.
